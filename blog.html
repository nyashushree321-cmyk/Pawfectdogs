Care guides

Short, vet-reviewed advice.

How much should I feed my dog?

Portion sizes depend on age, weight and activity. Start with the pack guide, then adjust every two weeks by checking your dog's waist and ribs.

Does my dog need a sweater?

Short-coated, small, senior and thin-coated dogs lose heat fast. If your dog shivers on walks, add a layer.

Choosing the right bed

Large and senior dogs do best on memory foam. Dogs that curl up like a rim; dogs that sprawl like a flat mat.

Vaccination schedule at a glance

Puppies start at 6 to 8 weeks with boosters every 3 to 4 weeks until 16 weeks, then yearly. Ask your vet to confirm.

Safe grooming at home

Brush weekly, bathe every 3 to 4 weeks with dog shampoo, and trim nails monthly.
